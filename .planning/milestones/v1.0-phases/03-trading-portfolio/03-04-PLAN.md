<objective>
**As a** FinAlly user, **I want to** watch a chart of my total portfolio value over time that gains
new points as time passes and immediately after every trade, **so that** I can see whether my
decisions are making the portfolio grow.

time to be visible. 03-01 already writes the post-trade snapshot inside the trade transaction; this
plan adds the periodic writer, the read endpoint, and the chart that makes both visible.

snapshot task in the app lifespan, and `PnLChart.tsx` mounted in the terminal.
